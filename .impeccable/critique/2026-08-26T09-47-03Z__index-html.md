## Overall Impression

The page has a real point of view — the mono-readout "instrument" identity is doing genuine brand work — but two mobile scroll-hijacking mechanics fight the reader for control, and a handful of concrete defects (a dead mobile-nav focus trap, truncated case-study sentences, a vanishing desktop nav) sit right next to the site's strongest asset. Biggest opportunity: the site's own promise is "numbers, not promises," yet it ships fabricated case-study percentages, a placeholder P.IVA, and mid-sentence-truncated proof copy on the same homepage.

## What's Working

- **The mono "readout" system** — every measured claim renders in JetBrains Mono / Instrument Blue / tabular-nums, visually distinct from Inter prose.
- **The "joined ledger" case-study grid** — shared border with internal dividers instead of floating cards, reading like a report line-item.
- **Disciplined interaction restraint** — shadows only on hover/focus-visible, hover gated behind `(hover:hover)`, buttons snap on `:active`.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Scroll control taken from the user twice on mobile**
Why it matters: the roadmap pin-and-translate and the 11-row service scroll-lock (350ms body overflow:hidden per row) both remove native scroll from a busy phone user.
Fix: replace the roadmap pin with a native swipeable carousel with visible dots; drop the touch scroll-lock on service rows.
Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P1] Mobile nav overlay is a keyboard focus trap**
Why it matters: `#mobile-nav` hides only via opacity/pointer-events with no inert/aria-hidden toggle — keyboard/screen-reader users tab into 7 invisible links first. Real WCAG 2.4.3 violation.
Fix: toggle `inert` (or aria-hidden + tabindex="-1" on links) when `#mobile-nav` lacks `.open`.
Suggested command: /impeccable audit

**[P1] Desktop primary nav disappears after 8px of scroll**
Why it matters: `.site-header.is-scrolled .nav-desktop{display:none}` fires almost immediately, leaving only CTA+hamburger for the whole scrolled experience.
Fix: keep the link pill persistently visible at desktop widths (>=981px); reserve the merge for constrained viewports.
Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P2] Case-study teasers truncate mid-clause with a bare ellipsis**
Why it matters: reads as broken content; compounds with the placeholder P.IVA to signal unfinished data on a "precision" brand.
Fix: hand-write teaser copy ending on a complete clause, or line-clamp with an explicit "Leggi tutto" affordance.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P2] Heading level skipped (H2 -> H4) in two sections**
Why it matters: problem-list and roadmap steps jump to H4 with no H3 — screen-reader heading navigation loses the outline at the core narrative.
Fix: promote to H3 (adjust font-size utility if needed).
Suggested command: /impeccable audit
